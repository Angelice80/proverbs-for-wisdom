    From the page to the video

    You can't click a printed book, so every lesson gets a short web address and a QR code.

    1. Find the code at the end of the lesson.
    2. Scan it with a phone camera, or type the short address.
    3. Jerry's video for that verse opens.

    The QR codes in this preview already work. The short address starts working once the domain is set up.

    Hi, I'm Jerry

    I spent thirty years as an engineer. Now that I'm retired, I spend my time on something I care about even more: helping young people grow in wisdom.

    Proverbs is full of short, practical advice for everyday life. I wrote these books so parents, grandparents and teachers can sit down with a young person and work through it one verse at a time. Family and church have always been at the center of my life, and I hope these books help yours.
